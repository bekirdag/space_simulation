CosmosMap Privacy Policy

Updated 23 September 2026 · applies to the CosmosMap iPad app and cosmosmap.org

CosmosMap is a free space simulation. It has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking across apps or websites.

Data we collect

CosmosMap does not ask for or store personal information. To understand how the website and the iPad app are used, we run our own privacy-friendly analytics (Rybbit, hosted on our server). It uses no cookies and no advertising identifiers, and it does not store your IP address. It records anonymous usage: pages and features used, approximate location (country/region, derived from the IP address, which is then discarded), device type, operating system and browser. This data is not linked to you, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Crash reports and usage statistics in the iPad app

The iPad app also uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google as our service provider (data processor). Crashlytics sends a crash report when the app crashes or hits a serious error: the technical state of the app at that moment, device model, iPadOS version, app version and diagnostic data such as memory and disk use. Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events, such as which planet or object you travel to, opening an object's info box, the length of a search (not its text), and quality or preset changes, together with approximate location (country/region) and device type. Both use a random identifier created by Firebase for this installation of the app; it is not your name, Apple ID, email, IP address, advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV), and it resets if you delete the app. The app does not include Apple's advertising framework, does not ask for tracking permission, and this data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising, ad personalisation or tracking, and is not sold. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Network requests

To show up-to-date planet positions and object descriptions, the app requests public astronomy data (NASA/JPL Horizons ephemerides and Wikipedia/NASA descriptions and images) through the cosmosmap.org server, which caches it. These requests contain only the object or date being looked up. Like any web server, cosmosmap.org receives your IP address as part of the connection; it is used only to deliver the response and in short-lived server logs for security and reliability, and is never used to identify or profile you.

On-device storage

Your settings and cached astronomy data are kept on your device only, so the app starts faster and works offline. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

CosmosMap was made for curious people of all ages, including children. It collects no personal information, only the anonymous usage statistics and crash reports described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: github.com/bekirdag/space_simulation/issues.
