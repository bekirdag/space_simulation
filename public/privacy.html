CosmosMap Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026 · applies to the CosmosMap iPad app and cosmosmap.org

CosmosMap is a free space simulation. It has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. CosmosMap does not ask for, collect, store or share personal information, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Network requests

To show up-to-date planet positions and object descriptions, the app requests public astronomy data (NASA/JPL Horizons ephemerides and Wikipedia/NASA descriptions and images) through the cosmosmap.org server, which caches it. These requests contain only the object or date being looked up. Like any web server, cosmosmap.org receives your IP address as part of the connection; it is used only to deliver the response and in short-lived server logs for security and reliability, and is never used to identify or profile you.

On-device storage

Your settings and cached astronomy data are kept on your device only, so the app starts faster and works offline. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

CosmosMap was made for curious people of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information, it is safe for children to use.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: github.com/bekirdag/space_simulation/issues.
