CosmosMap Support

CosmosMap is a free, real-time map of the Solar System, the Milky Way and nearby galaxies. There is no account to create and nothing to buy.

Getting around (iPad)

  • Rotate the view: drag with one finger.
  • Zoom: pinch.
  • Pan: drag with two fingers.
  • Select an object: tap it or its label. Fly to it: double-tap.
  • More options: touch and hold.
  • Use the search panel to find planets, moons, stars, nebulae and galaxies; tap ⓘ for a description.

Requirements

The iPad app requires iPadOS 26 or later (it uses WebGPU for 3D rendering). On the web, use a browser with WebGPU support at cosmosmap.org.
